Help and programmes

How payments are held

  • Made-to-order, service and bulk orders are confirmed by the seller first. Nothing is charged until they say yes.
  • You pay with GCash, Maya, QR Ph, card or online banking on the payment provider’s secure page. Iskaparate never sees your card or bank details.
  • Your money waits with Iskaparate until your order is delivered, picked up (by QR scan) or the service is done.
  • If nothing is reported within 3 days, the seller is paid. Report a problem in that window and the payment stays on hold until Support decides.
  • For made-to-order items, 30% of the seller’s share may be released as soon as you pay so they can buy ingredients and materials.

Shipping, pickup and returns

  • Courier rates are quoted at checkout by weight, size and destination. Each seller chooses which couriers to offer.
  • Local pickup or meet-up uses a time window. Show your pickup QR code, or read out the 6-digit code if you have no signal.
  • If you cancel before the seller starts or ships, you get a full refund. If the seller cancels or misses the promised date, you get a full refund.
  • If you cancel a made-to-order item after the advance was paid, the seller keeps the advance and the rest is refunded.
