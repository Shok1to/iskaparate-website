Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The Academy is free for Filipino micro-entrepreneurs, whether or not you sell on Iskaparate.

Do I need to be registered with BIR or DTI?

No. Many of our sellers start unregistered. One session explains when and how to register, and our mentors can help you with the paperwork.

I’m not good with technology. Can I still join?

Yes. If you can send a text and take a photo with your phone, you can follow every lesson. Sessions are in Filipino and English.

Will I get a shop on Iskaparate after?

Graduates who want to sell with us can apply as a vendor. Our team builds and runs your shop, so you don’t need to manage anything online.
