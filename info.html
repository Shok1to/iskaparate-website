Help and programmes

How paying works

  • Every order is confirmed by the vendor first. You are never charged for something a vendor can’t make or deliver.
  • Once the vendor says yes, you pay with GCash, Maya or InstaPay (a transfer from any bank app). We don’t take credit cards.
  • Your money waits with Iskaparate until your order is delivered.
  • If nothing is reported within 3 days of delivery, the vendor is paid. Report a problem in that window and the payment stays on hold until Support decides.
  • For made-to-order items, 30% of the vendor’s share may be released as soon as you pay so they can buy ingredients and materials.

Delivery and returns

  • Many vendors deliver orders themselves within their area. For everywhere else, Iskaparate books a courier.
  • The delivery fee is shown in your cart before you send your request.
  • Tap “I received it” when your order arrives. That starts the 3-day return window.
  • If the vendor cancels or misses the promised date, you get a full refund. If you cancel a made-to-order item after paying, the vendor keeps the 30% advance and the rest is refunded.

How we run vendors’ shops

Most of our vendors sell on Facebook and face to face, and don’t have time to manage another online store. So Iskaparate’s team does it for them: we photograph products, write listings, answer buyers and keep stock up to date. Vendors only confirm each order by text and deliver. Every vendor’s identity is checked with a valid government ID, such as a PhilSys national ID or senior citizen ID.

Finding items we don’t carry

If none of our vendors have what you need, tell our helper. Our team checks other trusted online shops, texts you the options and prices, and places the order for you once you choose and pay. The shop’s name isn’t shown in the options, but your order summary says clearly that the item comes from another shop and who to contact if something goes wrong.
